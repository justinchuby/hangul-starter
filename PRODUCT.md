# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Chinese-speaking adults beginning to learn Korean, often studying independently on a phone or desktop.

## Product Purpose

Teach the foundations of Hangul through core consonants, vowels, syllable construction, and short self-check quizzes. Success means a learner can recognize the selected letters and understand how they combine into readable syllable blocks.

## Positioning

A self-contained Chinese Hangul alphabet lesson that turns a learner's letter choices directly into syllable blocks and pronunciation guidance.

## Operating Context

The page is used for short, repeatable study sessions in a browser without accounts, setup, or external services.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with no build step or third-party dependencies. Learned letters and progress persist in browser localStorage. “Pinyin” in the learner's wording refers to Hangul letters and syllable combinations, not Chinese Hanyu Pinyin.

## Brand Commitments

Warm, distinctive, study-focused, and not overly decorative. The teaching language is concise Simplified Chinese.

## Evidence on Hand

No existing visual system, media assets, testimonials, commercial claims, or external learning content are available in the repository.

## Product Principles

- Let learners make and inspect real Hangul syllable blocks immediately.
- Keep pronunciation guidance useful without claiming exact equivalence to Chinese sounds.
- Reward steady recognition and practice rather than speed or streaks.
- Make the learning experience usable by keyboard and assistive technology.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Mobile-first responsive design, visible keyboard focus, semantic controls, ARIA live feedback, and color contrast that supports legible study in varied lighting.
